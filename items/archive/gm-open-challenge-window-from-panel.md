# Let the GM open the challenge window from the panel

The GM should be able to open the challenge window from the player panel, without going back to the journal.

**Decision (Justin, 2026-11-10):** for the GM, the challenge name in the panel header is a button that opens the challenge window. Players see plain text.

- Dragging the panel by its header must still work. A click without a drag opens the window.

**Done when:** on a live challenge, the GM's click on the name opens (or brings forward) the window, and a player's click does nothing.
