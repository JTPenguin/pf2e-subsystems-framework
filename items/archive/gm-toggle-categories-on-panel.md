# Let the GM turn weaknesses, resistances and Other on and off from the panel

The GM can click a revealed weakness, resistance or Other card on the panel to turn it on or off.

**Decisions (Justin, 2026-11-10):**

- A click flips On and Off only, as the GM window chip does. Spent stays a GM window action.
- Only the GM can click. Players see the cards as now.
- The panel shows only revealed cards, so hidden ones can't be toggled from it.

Builds on [[modifier-toggles]] (archived, done 2026-11-09).

**Done when:** on a live challenge, a GM click on a panel card changes its state in the GM window and on a player's panel.
