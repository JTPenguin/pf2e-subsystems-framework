# Split weaknesses and resistances into their own sections

Weaknesses and resistances should each have their own section in the GM window.

**Order (both GM window and panel):** resistances, then weaknesses, then Other.

**Decisions (Justin, 2026-11-10):**

- The GM window has three sections: Resistances, Weaknesses, Other.
- Other cards carry no kind label; the section heading is the label (see [[single-other-category-replaces-adjustments-penalties]]).

**Claude's defaults, unless Justin says otherwise:** an empty section is hidden in Run mode, and in Edit mode each section has its own Add button that creates an entry of that kind.

**Done when:** on a live challenge, both the GM window and the panel show the three groups in that order.
