# Fade inactive and spent cards on the panel, strongly, for everyone

On the panel, a resistance, weakness or Other card that is not active should fade, so the difference reads at a glance.

**Now:** an inactive card loses its color stripe, and that is the only change (see [[on-off-indicator-stripe-only-when-on]]). Spent cards are already dimmed on the panel.

**Decided (Justin, 2026-11-10):**

- **Everyone** sees the fade: players and GM.
- **Stronger than hidden items** in the GM window (those are 60%). Aim for about 40% opacity, so Off reads at a glance at the table.
- Inactive and Spent cards fade the same way.

**Watch for:** on the GM's panel a faded card is still clickable to turn it on; keep its hover visible so the GM can tell.

**Done when:** on a live challenge, an inactive card and a spent card are clearly faded next to an active one, for a player and for the GM, in both themes.
