# Use one representation for complications and opportunities

Complications and opportunities are currently both subjects and challenge-level modifiers. The idea: keep them only as challenge-level modifiers. Then a challenge has one type of subject, not several in a preset.

**To decide:** what breaks when they stop being subjects (presets, scoring, the panel, and existing challenge data). Justin wants this thought through before any build.
