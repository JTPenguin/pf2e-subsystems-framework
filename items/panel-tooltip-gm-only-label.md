# Panel card tooltips: drop the Player label, say GM Only on its own line

The hover text on panel cards shows a "Player" label. Players do not need it, so remove it from the player tooltip.

The GM tooltip label should read "GM Only" instead of "GM". It goes on its own line, separate from the card text.

**Done when:** a player's card tooltip shows no "Player" label, and the GM's card tooltip shows "GM Only" on a line of its own. Check both in Foundry on a live panel.
